0.28.0 — Varied Japanese practice with stable interaction

Objective
Help learners repeatedly understand and use the same word sense or grammar
function in different contexts, without repeatedly learning how to operate the
exercise. Preserve all enrolled identities, schedules and frozen pending work.

Implementation contracts
1. Reviewed practice material is a separately versioned private content-pack
   namespace. It binds to an exact existing lesson identity/content fingerprint.
   Existing template indices and stored question snapshots retain their meaning.
   Clean MIT source builds without that private namespace.
2. New templates reuse choice, text and reorder interaction modes and existing
   ability identities. Task families describe language use (context, dialogue,
   information, completion), not additional independent SRS cards.
3. A template may carry practice schema version 1, task_family, context_id,
   optional example_id, scaffold (recognition/cued/retrieval), target_spans,
   background_ids and reviewed status. Spans refer to the public prompt.
   Example association alone never licenses guessing distractors or judging an
   arbitrary response as wrong. Reviewed answers and conservative unresolved
   handling remain authoritative.
4. Future-question selection uses bounded recent evidence, response support,
   task/context coverage and enabled kinds. Already pending work is not replaced.
   Prefer supported practice after difficulty, progressively test independent
   retrieval, and retain diversity rather than chasing only old mistakes.
5. Guided demonstration and practice do not produce formal FSRS grades. The
   existing exactly-once contract for a frozen formal attempt (including reveal
   or a hint) is preserved. A choice success cannot create a text-recall success.
6. New guided exercises use their actual input operation, with an explicit short
   demonstration/help. Familiarity selection, prerequisite introductions and
   continuous progression remain available. Drafts must survive restart.
7. Japanese stimulus precedes secondary controls. Short familiar instructions
   remain where needed; avoid mysterious icons. Answer feedback, safe audio,
   explanation and four formal review ratings remain available.
8. Ruby, pitch, TTS and background help obey the tested ability's anti-leakage
   rules. Unknown prerequisite content is supported or excluded; it is not
   silently graded, enrolled or used to penalize unrelated memory units.

Increments and checks
A. Content/template extension and conservative grading: schema/coverage checks,
   answer acceptance, invalid variants, private/demo separation.
B. Selection and evidence: bounded history, deterministic recovery, diversity,
   enabled-kind restrictions, retrieval coverage, old queue/due preservation.
C. First learning and presentation: real guided controls, continuous flow,
   snapshot/draft resume, no answer leakage, stable control placement.
D. Integration: targeted regression suites and finite synthetic sessions;
   isolated Android manual checks at normal/enlarged text, first learning,
   correct/wrong/reveal, new contexts, interruption and resumption.
E. Freeze tested sources; build personal, QA and clean demo distributions;
   verify APK inventories and package identity, archive reviewed outputs and
   commit code and artifacts separately.

Scope boundaries
Do not restart the paused four-year/full simulation, operate on a real learner
database, call a paid model, or work on the deferred mobile download issue.
Coverage reports must distinguish audited generated practice from manually
authored scenarios, and actual content coverage from supported type names.
No universal optimal practice count or FSRS weight is claimed.

Evidence informing the design
Frances et al. (2020), contextual diversity in vocabulary learning:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7435265/
Suzuki & Sunada (2019), L2 practice type and practice schedule:
https://yuichisuzuki.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Suzuk-Sunada-2019-SSLA.pdf
NSW CESE, Cognitive load theory in practice:
https://education.nsw.gov.au/about-us/education-data-and-research/cese/publications/practical-guides-for-educators/cognitive-load-theory-in-practice.html
Japan Foundation, Irodori:
https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/en/about.html

These sources support context diversity, actual practice of the desired skill,
worked examples and gradual removal of assistance. Specific runtime selection
weights and UI arrangements are product choices, requiring verification.

Implemented coverage and limits
- 4,911 appended templates across 343 existing word senses and 324 grammar
  items, without changing their IDs. 96 manually authored tasks include deeper
  situation/dialogue/transformation practice for 28 grammar items plus 12 word
  information-extraction tasks. The remaining breadth reuses exact reviewed
  sentence contexts; it does not claim all 324 grammar items have every mode.
- 1,318 additional annotation sentences; 35 confirmed reading corrections and
  explicit uncertainty for names or accents that could not be verified.
- Ordinary selection uses at most 24 recent observations per ability. Desktop
  measurements are not a claim about phone latency. Extreme reconstruction of
  500 entirely missing queues remains a separate batch-performance opportunity.

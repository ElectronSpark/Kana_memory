0.28.1 — Extend context variety across the existing authored course

Scope
All 353 authored word senses and 324 non-pronunciation grammar items receive
at least three task families in at least three distinct source sentences.
This is course coverage, not coverage of every entry in the dictionary.
The 0.28.0 interaction, memory identity and preservation contracts still apply.

Vocabulary
- Interpret the highlighted word in a sentence.
- Supply the word in a different sentence, with a short meaning cue.
- Recall the highlighted reading when it is not already printed.
- Select the conventional written form from its reading in context.
- Recall the base form of an inflected word when the answer is hidden.
- For words written in kana, select the sentence meaning using reviewed
  minimal contrasts rather than copying a visible reading.
Only applicable operations are used. Surface readings must be supported for
the actual inflection; a lemma reading cannot be attached to a different form.

Grammar
- Retain existing meaning/function questions and specialist authored scenarios.
- Complete four additional reviewed sentences with the target construction.
- Reconstruct those sentences using two to six word groups.
Chinese cues identify the intended meaning; Japanese groups keep whole words
and the relevant construction intact. Valid but unlisted alternatives remain
unresolved, not automatically wrong. These tasks do not claim exhaustive
semantic equivalence or independently authored novel Japanese sentences.

Selection and progress
New tasks reuse existing ability units. A task family or context is not a new
card, and merely showing an example is not a review grade. Enabled question
kinds and prerequisite support continue to constrain selection. A bounded
deterministic rotating tie-breaker prevents low-index variants from starving
other contexts after repeated short sessions. It stores no extra history and
does not alter due times. Frozen questions, drafts and completed grades remain
authoritative across upgrades; old template arrays remain exact prefixes.

Presentation and content separation
No new answer controls or extra question-selection menus. Japanese uses the
study font; instructions and Chinese reconstruction cues use the UI font.
Ruby/TTS obey the tested ability's existing answer-leakage rules. New gap
annotations retain unchanged segments from their complete original sentence;
segments crossing the gap, disputed readings and unknown accents stay pending.
Private JSON authoring, reviewed source hashes and compiled material stay in
the content/artifact repository. The MIT app builds without them. Local tools
used to prepare the pack do not add tokenizers or neural models to the phone.

Verification
Coverage/source-anchor validation, conservative grading, stable prefix and
legacy fallback checks; finite mixed-mode/short-session selection tests;
isolated emulator checks for new controls, correct/wrong/reveal, first learning,
interruption/restart, original queue/draft preservation and enlarged text.
The paused multi-year simulation, paid API and mobile download work are outside
this increment. Release evidence records actual tested cases and limitations.

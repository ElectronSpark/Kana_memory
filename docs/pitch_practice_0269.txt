KanaMemory 0.26.9 — pronunciation feedback
=========================================

The seven submitted reports concern repeated conceptual questions, unclear
accent terminology, and a lack of hands-on mora/pitch exercises. Raw reports,
screenshots and learner context remain private and are not release artifacts.

Interaction
-----------
New authored pronunciation templates retain the two existing ability IDs.
pitch_fill asks the learner to apply a stated accent to one real word with an
optional particle. High/Low consumes a supplied mora in elementary mode, or a
kana character in learner-segmentation mode. Merge consumes the next small kana
and joins it to the previous group; Backspace reverses one learner action.
Single-word exercises give the word/particle boundary automatically, including
editable old snapshots which originally requested Split. Their high/low and
small-kana grouping inputs are preserved. Automatic boundaries do not require
an extra Backspace. No arbitrary speech segmentation is inferred.
The v1 contract deliberately covers one word plus an optional particle; a
multiword sentence must use a future explicit format rather than guessing.

pitch_identify presents the contour and asks separately for the word's mora
count and accent number. Particles are visibly distinct and excluded from the
word count. Flat/final-drop ambiguity without a following particle is rejected.
An unknown word's dictionary accent is not inferred from its spelling.

Kana stay on a common baseline; high/low horizontal marks appear above them.
The current character/group is highlighted, and fixed token colors distinguish
words and particles. Glyph positions never depend on pitch or entered groups.
Wrapping prefers complete words, keeping short following particles with their
word when possible. Overlong words use a deterministic character fallback,
with continued token color and line connections. Merge and undo only update
marks, group spans and progress, never lift or lower the kana themselves.
The answer controls use upper/lower line symbols, a join symbol and Backspace;
Android accessibility descriptions and long-press hints explain the symbols.
Android draws the pitch diagram in one retained vector Canvas. Input updates
its marks, emphasis and accessible description without rebuilding native
layout containers. Text keeps the same point size as native lesson labels.
Additional examples are available without repeating the primary diagram.
Word ruby elsewhere retains the existing -/_ and accent-number preferences.

Storage and assessment
----------------------
pitch_practice.version=1 specifies the exact kana, authored morae, accent,
particle and editor options. Responses have a pp1: JSON envelope; controls and
feedback show diagrams and readable labels, never that serialized value.
Drafts contain reversible actions or two selections and a matching response.
Draft operations, instruction and guided practice do not create review events.
Only a completed independent submission is graded, with the existing one-event
per-question finalization. Revealing the answer remains assisted.
Inline guide feedback is brought into view after layout settles; delayed
scrolling is ignored if the page, question, result or scroll container changed.
Splitting errors are diagnosed before pitch differences caused by misalignment.

Existing study IDs, unit identities, schedules, stored questions and drafts are
retained. Exact audited lesson overlays improve presentation and future
question generation without mutating enrolled content. Existing question
snapshots and answered guides remain recoverable in their original form.
New-format snapshots, drafts and guide checkpoints are validated on insertion
and backup import. Imported/custom material cannot impersonate a reviewed
built-in teaching record by reusing only its ID.

Answer protection
-----------------
Identification diagrams expose no accent number or explicit word-count label.
Fill diagrams do not complete missing levels. Generic ruby and TTS hints remain
blocked for pronunciation questions. Ordinary TTS is not a verified reference
for a specific accent, including on the result page. These are annotation and
application exercises, not listening discrimination or speech assessment.

Feedback capture
----------------
A report made from inside the feedback editor now retains one bounded original
page context. The editor's actual screen is still captured; the underlying
lesson is available to maintenance without recursive capture growth or exposing
hidden solutions in the on-device capture preview.

Validation scope
----------------
Use targeted domain/storage/controller/layout regressions, synthetic isolated
Android QA and reproducible package checks. The paused four-year simulation is
not restarted for this increment. No real learner database is edited for tests.

Scope and policy
The audit covers all 524 bundled courses: 324 grammar, 34 pronunciation and
166 word senses. The full learning path retains all 850 stable entry identities,
including 241 playable kana directions and 85 references. The previous kana
graph and same-script independent-success gate remain in force.

Hard dependencies represent forms or semantic distinctions needed to answer a
course's questions. The new target itself belongs in its introduction. Merely
occurring in an example is not sufficient reason to require a separate course:
incidental vocabulary receives safe local assistance instead.

The course graph grows from 596 to 1047 direct edges. Additions affect 118
courses; 208 ancestor closures expand. Examples include all applicable te/nai
forms, plain verb/adjective/copula forms, transitivity, contraction source
constructions and grammatical functions of pronunciation particles. A complete
structural audit finds no missing node or cycle. Source references and the
reviewed form-consumer lists are in course_prerequisites.py and the graph JSON.
These sources support Japanese usage; the precise prerequisite order is the
app's teaching decision, not an official curriculum mandated by those sources.

Automatic selection and continuation
Selecting a new item traverses already-reserved but unstarted ancestors, then
adds only missing identities. Browsing and previewing remain read-only. Existing
successful study, real drafts, ungraded feedback and explicit prior-knowledge
acknowledgments keep their route. Playing audio or storing empty input is not
evidence of an answer and no longer bypasses a prerequisite gate.

For an untouched saved copy of an exact bundled lesson, runtime admission
uses the current additional prerequisites without rewriting its original lesson,
question or draft. Source IDs, material fingerprint and abilities must match;
an unrelated custom/AI item cannot become trusted merely by using the same ID.
Continue appends missing prerequisites once. Each required course must receive
a genuine unassisted successful primary rating before unlocking its successor;
reading, guided practice and an ungraded correct answer do not create evidence.
Later lapses do not revoke a previously established foundation.

An old custom branch with missing or cyclic dependencies remains unresolved
and blocked; it cannot prevent unrelated valid courses from continuing. Explicit
new enrollment still rejects invalid dependencies. No fake content is created.
The fully closed-graph cache checks saved membership and external DB changes;
local hot checks for all 524 courses take about 5–6 ms, versus 245–280 ms without
the cache. These desktop timings are not phone performance guarantees.

Section and unit grouping follows the corrected topology. Entry identities,
learning records and reservations survive regrouping; derived section/unit IDs
and counts may differ. No content database or learner schema migration occurs.

Question context and non-disclosure
The audit enumerates 1674 local question variants. Safe non-target annotation
is available in 961 of 983 cloze/usage variants. Exact local vocabulary senses
and 97 manually reviewed public prompts supply short lexical context; 732 of
1302 grammar meaning/cloze variants have such help, with 1093 individual glosses.
Known background word senses can suppress unnecessary hints. Japanese spans
retain their study typography, distinct from Chinese instructions.

The manual context set covers 50 course groups, including basic place/action
sentences and decisive time, role or lexical cues such as 毎朝, アレルギー,
目覚まし and 案じる. Meanings of base verbs do not translate the tested tense,
aspect, voice or modality. A work-description scene disambiguates the habitual
use of a progressive-form example sentence. New progressive/result meaning choices use
parallel abstract functions so a background noun cannot select the answer.

The old mu-te-form production question also required ください. New questions
ask only for the verb form. Exact unchanged old questions keep their original
answer and receive a note identifying the fixed given ending. Display support
for saved questions is read-only, anchored to the exact bundled target, variant,
prompt, task, answer and options. It cannot alter stored scoring or manufacture
trusted hints for imported/edited questions.

Target text, equivalent readings and answer-bearing hints remain excluded.
Cloze questions remain silent; annotation does not enable TTS to read a hidden
answer. Reading/pitch/listening answers are not disclosed. Uncertain meanings
are not guessed from a dictionary's first sense. Unmatched background tokens
are not all missing dependencies: many cloze tasks already provide Chinese
context, and some apparent tokens are the form being taught. The counts above
are coverage evidence, not a claim that every possible Japanese example has
received exhaustive semantic certification.

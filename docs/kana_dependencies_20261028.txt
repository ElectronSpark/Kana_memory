Kana prerequisite audit and repair — 0.22.14 / 220014

Problem and policy
The content's components were correct, but every kana catalogue entry had an
empty dependency list. Mixed adaptive dispatch could consequently offer a
voiced contraction before its voiced constituent.

The structural audit covers 165 content rows / 326 script identities. The
playable graph contains 241 identities and 247 direct edges. Dependencies use
actual glyphs, not equivalent pronunciations, and never cross writing systems.
Examples: ぎゃ needs ぎ and や; ぎ needs き. ぴょ needs ぴ and よ;
ぴ needs ひ, not び. ティ needs テ and イ. Small reference glyphs expand to
their playable full-size ancestors; the contraction introduction teaches size
and mora grouping. Reference and historical symbols do not become audio quizzes.
The full graph and expanded reference paths are recorded in the graph JSON.

A same-script, independent correct primary review with rating above Again
unlocks a prerequisite. Introduction exposure, familiarity self-report, word
evidence, revealed answers and accepted homophone substitutions do not.
Later lapses do not revoke an established prerequisite. Previously assessed
advanced targets keep their review route, as do real drafts and ungraded results.

Admission, dispatch and preservation
New selection includes its exact prerequisite closure while retaining catalogue
and section IDs. Continuing an old queue appends missing predecessors once,
using enabled and available directions. The unchanged original queue is retained.
Dispatch gates all untouched targets, including the fast path and first-teaching
path; blocked items never become consumed/skipped history. A failed predecessor
retains its original card and due date. A paused predecessor remains paused.
Home distinguishes ready, timed and prerequisite-blocked counts. A blocked-only
queue has a saved waiting screen without a fabricated timer or completion claim.
No content database or learner schema migration is involved.

Evidence lookup uses indexed existence queries and the existing precise
connection revision cache. Checkpoint/draft writes do not invalidate evidence.
Tests cover event/card mutation, rollback, replacement and outside connections.
In desktop SQLite with 300,000 events, typical cold lookup fell from 624 ms to
0.59 ms; hot lookup was about 0.0045 ms. Worst synthetic never-passed histories
still cost 132–201 ms cold. These are local measurements, not phone guarantees.

Validation
The comprehensive selection contains 918 tests. Its initial run passed 910;
8 old assertions expected no added prerequisites. Those fixtures were updated
to require exact prerequisite closure, retained original questions/feedback and
no repeat insertion. The final affected selection passed all 167 tests. Together
this verifies all 918 distinct tests; the overlapping counts are not summed.
Separate full-graph checks verify every edge and every selectable identity.

Native checks with a synthetic disposable learner confirmed draft preservation, one-time predecessor insertion and that only an unassisted correct answer releases blocked targets.
